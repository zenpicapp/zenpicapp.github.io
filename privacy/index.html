Privacy Policy

Effective: November 7, 2026   Last updated: November 7, 2026

简体中文Support

The short version

  • Your photos and videos are never sent to us. Sorting and analysis happen on your iPhone, and we have no servers that receive them.
  • There's no account, no ads, no third-party analytics and no automatic crash reporting in the app.
  • Maps, place names and address search come from Apple, so the related coordinates and the text you type go to Apple.
  • We only receive what's in an email if you choose to write to us.

1. Who we are

ZenPic (淘片, “the app”) is developed and run by an independent developer, Yi Liang (梁毅). “We” and “us” mean the developer. Questions? Email 61073198@qq.com.

2. Your photos and videos

Once you allow access, the app reads the photos and videos you've made available, along with details like capture date, dimensions, duration, favorite status, albums and capture location. It uses these to show your library, group items, find duplicates and similar photos, sort screenshots, make PDFs and compress videos.

Image and text recognition use iOS system frameworks and run entirely on your iPhone. Recognized text isn't saved; only the resulting category is kept.

Favorites, album changes, edits, deletions you confirm, and newly compressed videos are written to your system photo library. You can change or turn off photo access in iOS Settings at any time. If you allow only selected photos, the app can work with only those.

3. What's stored on your iPhone

The app keeps the following on your iPhone. None of it is sent to us.

Included in iOS backups

If you use iCloud Backup or back up to a computer, these go into your own backup, which we can't read.

  • Sorting records: keep and Trash marks, album settings, where you left off, and compressed videos waiting for your decision. Reset App in Settings clears these.
  • Location table: the capture coordinates already attached to your photos, with each photo's library identifier, saved so the map opens faster. This is separate from your current location in section 5.
  • Place names: names already looked up, so they don't need to be looked up again.
  • App settings and your daily usage count.
  • PDFs you make: stored in the app's Documents folder and visible in the Files app under On My iPhone › ZenPic › PDF. You can delete them in the app or in Files.

Excluded from backups

  • Offline cache: smaller copies of your photos, not backups of your originals. Clear them on the Offline Cache page; your library isn't affected.
  • Analysis results: file sizes, similar-photo groups, screenshot categories, meme detection and daily sorting stats.
  • Diagnostic records: see section 8.

4. Maps and address search

The map, place names and address search all come from Apple Maps. When you open the map, the visible area and the coordinates of places on screen (used to look up their names) are sent to Apple. When you search, the text you type, as you type it, and the current map area are also sent to Apple. When you open the Info page of a photo or video with a location, its coordinates are also sent to Apple to look up the place name. Your photos and videos themselves are not sent.

These requests go directly to Apple and we don't receive them. The app doesn't save your searches. Nothing is sent unless you open the map or an Info page. Place-name lookups already in the queue may finish after you leave the map. To learn how Apple handles this information, see Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Your current location

The app asks for location access, While Using the App only, when you tap the location button on the map. It then gets your current location once, accurate to about 100 meters, and moves the map there. If you don't tap the button, you won't be asked. If you say no, the map, address search and sorting all still work.

The app doesn't save this location or send it to us, and it's cleared when you leave the map. The app never asks for Always access and doesn't track your location in the background. iOS and Apple's location services handle the location request itself. You can change this in iOS Settings at any time.

6. iCloud Photos

If an original is in iCloud, iOS may need to download it when you view it full size, edit it, make a PDF, save it for offline use or compress a video. Apple handles these downloads, they don't pass through us, and they may use cellular data. Offline caching uses Wi-Fi only by default, and you can change that on the Offline Cache page. Before compressing a video that needs downloading, the app shows you the size and waits for your OK.

The app has no cloud sync of its own. Changes to your library and newly compressed videos sync to your other devices according to your iCloud Photos settings.

7. Emails you send us

Feedback and Request a Feature don't go through any server of ours. Tapping send opens the iOS mail composer, where you can see the sending address, subject, message and attachments, and can edit or cancel. Nothing is sent until you tap Send there.

  • Feedback: your device model, iOS version and app version are added to the end of the message, and the screen shows you this before you write. You can attach up to 3 images you pick.
  • Request a Feature: contains only what you type.
  • No mail account on your iPhone: the app copies the subject and message to your clipboard for you to send yourself. Images aren't included.

Once you send a message, we receive your email address (and display name, if any), your message and any attachments. We use them only to reply, investigate problems and consider what you've asked for. We don't use them for marketing or advertising, sell them, or share them with anyone else. Our email provider delivers and stores messages for us; it handles them only to provide email service, with protection no weaker than this policy.

We keep messages for up to 12 months after an issue is resolved, then delete them. You can ask to see or delete yours at any time. We may confirm the sending address first so we don't delete someone else's messages. Please don't send passwords, card details or other people's private photos.

8. Diagnostic records

The app keeps diagnostic records on your iPhone for duplicate analysis and video compression, to help work out why something failed. They include device model, iOS and app versions, photo library identifiers, file sizes, video settings, timing, results and system error messages. They contain no photo or video content, but a system error message might include a file path.

These records are never sent automatically. You can view and copy them in Settings › Diagnostics, check them first, and decide whether to paste them into an email. Video compression keeps the 7 most recent files (one per day, up to 400 entries each). Duplicate analysis saves one record per run; these are removed when you delete the app.

9. Sorting reminders

The app asks for notification permission only when you turn on Sorting Reminders in Settings. After that, your iPhone shows a reminder at 8 p.m. each day. It doesn't involve any server, and you can turn it off at any time.

10. Sharing

Photos, PDFs and progress posters are shared through the iOS share sheet. You choose where they go, and the service you choose handles them from there.

11. Purchases and daily limits

Apple handles the annual subscription, lifetime purchase, Restore Purchases and offer codes. The app only reads from Apple what you've bought, to know whether Premium is active. We never see your card details or Apple Account information, and we don't run our own verification server. The sales reports Apple gives us are aggregated and don't identify you.

Your daily usage count stays on your iPhone and isn't sent to us.

12. Statistics from Apple

As with other apps on the App Store, Apple gives developers aggregated download and usage statistics. If you've chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, we may also see crash reports. Apple collects and manages this data, and we don't use it to identify you. You can turn sharing off in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

13. How to remove this information

  • Photo, location and notification permissions can all be changed in iOS Settings.
  • Reset App clears sorting records only. It doesn't clear the offline cache, PDFs, diagnostic records, the location table or place names, and it never deletes photos from your library.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your iPhone, including PDFs, so save any you want first. Offloading the app keeps its documents and data. Photos in your library, exported files, emails you've sent and existing backups aren't affected.
  • There's no account to close.

14. Children

The app isn't designed specifically for children, and we don't knowingly collect children's personal information. If you're a parent and your child has written to us, contact us and we'll delete the message.

15. Changes and contact

If features or data handling change, we'll update this page and the date above. This website is a static page with no analytics or advertising cookies. The hosting provider may keep routine access logs (IP address and time).

Contact: 61073198@qq.com
