简体中文Privacy Policy

Contact us

Email 61073198@qq.com and tell us what happened, what you expected and roughly what you tapped. If you attach screenshots, please cover faces, addresses, phone numbers and anything else private first.

You can also write from inside the app: Settings › Feedback or Request a Feature. Feedback automatically includes your device model, iOS version and app version so we can look into it, and you can see all of this before sending. If there's no mail account on your iPhone, the text is copied to your clipboard so you can paste it into any email app.

What devices does ZenPic run on?

iPhone only, running iOS 18.0 or later. No account needed.

Is ZenPic free?

Every feature is free to use, with a daily limit: 100 photos or videos a day, 300 on your first day, resetting at midnight your time. You can also keep up to 200 photos offline.

Swiping past a photo, deleting an item in Clean Up, handling a photo in Similar Photos, adding a photo to a PDF and compressing a video each count as one. Viewing photos, tapping Favorite, restoring from Trash, and browsing the map or your stats don't count.

ZenPic Premium removes these limits and comes as an annual subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Both work with Family Sharing. The app shows the current price before you buy.

How do I restore a purchase, redeem a code or cancel?

  • Restore or redeem: go to Me › ZenPic Premium. Restoring requires the Apple Account you bought with.
  • Cancel or manage a subscription: open the iPhone Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. Deleting the app doesn't cancel a subscription.
  • Refunds: Apple handles these at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Bought Premium and it isn't showing? Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account and connected to the internet, then tap Restore Purchases. Please don't send us your card number, Apple Account password or offer codes.

Where do photos go when I mark them for deletion?

Photos you toss while sorting go to ZenPic's Trash. They're still in your photo library, and you can restore them in the app at any time. When you tap Delete in Trash, iOS asks you to confirm. After that, they move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Items you delete directly in Clean Up skip ZenPic's Trash, but they still need the iOS confirmation and still go to Recently Deleted.

Why didn't I get more free space after deleting?

Items in Recently Deleted still take up space until you empty it or 30 days pass. With Optimize iPhone Storage on, your iPhone mostly holds smaller copies, so deleting mainly frees iCloud space. iOS decides how free space on the phone changes.

Can I sort offline?

Yes, if you prepare ahead. While you're online, save the photos you want on the Offline Cache page. With no signal you can then keep them, toss them or add them to albums. Offline copies are smaller versions, not backups of your originals. Editing, playing full videos that aren't downloaded, downloading iCloud originals, maps and address search still need a connection. Clearing the cache doesn't touch your library. Caching uses Wi-Fi only by default; you can change that on the same page.

Does compressing a video delete the original?

Not automatically. The compressed version is saved as a new video and the original stays where it is. On the review screen, watch both, then delete the original, keep both, or discard the copy. If you choose to delete something, iOS asks you to confirm. If compression fails or you cancel, the original isn't affected.

How much you save depends on the video's length and format and on your device. Space only comes back after the original leaves Recently Deleted, and with Optimize iPhone Storage on, most of the savings are in iCloud.

How do the map, search and location differ?

The map uses the capture locations already in your photos, so photos without a location won't appear on it. The map and place names come from Apple Maps and need a connection; the coordinates of places on screen are sent to Apple, and so are a photo or video's coordinates when you open its Info page. Address search sends the text you type to Apple. Your current location is used only when you tap the location button, and the app doesn't save it. You can view the map and search without granting location access.

Where are my PDFs?

In PDF Archive in the app, or in the Files app under On My iPhone › ZenPic › PDF. Deleting the app also deletes these PDFs, so save or share any you want to keep first.

How do I turn reminders on or off?

Go to Settings › Sorting Reminders. The first time you turn it on, iOS asks to allow notifications. You'll then get a reminder at 8 p.m. each day.

What does Reset App clear?

Only your sorting records: keep and Trash marks, album settings and the like. It never deletes photos from your library, and it doesn't clear the offline cache or your PDFs.

How do I send you diagnostics?

Go to Settings › Diagnostics, tap Copy, and paste into your email. If a compression fails, the details are also on the result screen. Feel free to read them first: they include photo library identifiers and system error messages, which may contain a file path, but no photo content. The app never sends these on its own.

How quickly will I hear back?

ZenPic is made by one independent developer. We'll reply as soon as we can, but we can't promise a set time. To see how your message is handled, read section 7 of the Privacy Policy.
